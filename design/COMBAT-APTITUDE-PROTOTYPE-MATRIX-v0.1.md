# v0.3 Combat & Aptitude Prototype Matrix v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Status:** Playtest matrix, not class canon

| Aptitude | Weapon | Primary | Defense | Gate-2 technique | Field utility | Main question |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fighter | Sword | Slash | Guard | Lunge | obstacle/brace/general safety | Is the baseline readable and satisfying? |
| Scout | Spear | Thrust | Brace | Sweep | terrain probe/route signs | Does reach feel different without becoming free safety? |
| Ranger | Bow | Shot | Evade Step | Aimed Shot | tracking/signaling | Is ranged play deliberate without trivializing enemies? |
| Mage | Staff/Focus | Impulse Pattern | Ward | Impulse Burst | Sensing/Pattern diagnosis | Can bounded magic feel distinct without a Mana subsystem? |

## Shared constraints

- same enemy health for first comparison;
- same map/obstacles;
- similar equipment workmanship;
- no elemental weakness;
- no class-exclusive quest completion;
- same healing access baseline;
- test one loadout at a time before party synergies.

## First comparison scene

Use one compact field:
- one open approach;
- one narrow obstacle lane;
- one retreat route;
- one light creature;
- one creature with a readable charge/approach;
- one noncombat clue/utility interaction.

This lets the test compare:
- sword arc;
- spear lane;
- bow line of sight;
- Pattern range/control;
- defense;
- retreat;
- field utility.

## Playtest prompts

After each loadout ask:

1. What did this weapon want you to do differently?
2. When did you feel committed/unsafe?
3. Was defense understandable?
4. Did range feel earned?
5. Could you retreat deliberately?
6. Which noncombat utility did you notice?
7. Did the aptitude feel like a starting background or a permanent class prison?
8. Would you willingly try another weapon on the same character?

## Acceptance

A loadout passes when:
- testers can describe its unique play pattern without reading the design doc;
- it can complete ordinary F work;
- it has at least one reason to use its defense;
- it does not invalidate another loadout by range/safety alone;
- controls fit the shared input budget.

Do not choose a "winner" weapon. The goal is distinct viable languages.
