# First ten hours — a professional life, not a fixed quest order

**Status:** Pacing hypothesis. Wall-clock ranges guide playtests, not timed progression locks. Reading speed, aptitude and optional social activity vary.

## Starting state and first hour

Player arrives on foot near Ternhaven's market with 60c, basic clothing and no special reputation. Familiar background work explains practical competence without secret royalty. View the Guild, river mill, inn and dungeon gate from connected paths.

Preserve v0.1's hour: arrival 0–10 minutes; registration 10–20; job choice 20–30; field work 30–45; return around 45–60. Reception lends aptitude-appropriate gear and explains safety, fees and one-above permits in usable pieces. Four slice aptitudes can be tried in the yard before confirming; this does not delete v0.1's six broader archetypes.

Meet N01 Mira, N03 Dessa and one novice; see N05 Ressa's inn and a veteran return without stopping for a long lore lecture. Post delivery, gathering, search and survey choices. Complete or honestly retreat from one contract and receive a comprehensible ledger. Reception's first recognition is “you reported what you saw,” not “you are destined to save us.”

## Progression bands

| Play time | Places and social contacts | Work / resources | New capability and recognition | Choices / safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1h | Ternhaven, Guild, road edge; N01/N03/N05 plus peer | First 60–90c job or safe recovery; first return report | F license, loan kit, route sketch | No mandatory correct first job; loan gear prevents food-versus-weapon lock |
| 1–3h | Aldermead, Brackenwood edge; N10/N07/N15/N18 | Two or three varied F contracts; food, bunk, repair budgeting | First permanent weapon around 2–4h if saving; recognition by one local | Upgrade timing depends on surplus; a failure has safe work alternative |
| 3–5h | Siltwell exterior then approved interior; N12/N19/N21 | Q11 then permitted Q13; share agreement and preparation | First real dungeon outing, light/retreat competence | A player can delay the dungeon for surface jobs; mentor conditions remain explicit |
| 5–7h | Underfold floor 1 marked loop; N08/N16/N27 | Route log, archive sample, optional social or investigation jobs | Information becomes useful gear; one believable NPC story/state change becomes visible | Deep floors wait for permission, not player curiosity alone |
| 7–10h | Exam route, Guild celebration or feedback; returning companions | Varied evidence + 30 merit; exam; E jobs after pass | F → E practical recognition, modest equipment/security buffer | No guarantee by hour 10; retake after practice, no exam fee |

Floors 2–3 are available for later slice sessions or efficient approved parties; **not every first-ten-hour route must clear them**. Floor 3 is a slice-capstone expedition, not an F → E exam requirement. This prevents content scope from dictating beginner pacing.

## Three valid routes

**Civic investigator:** Q01 → Q02 → Q03 → Q04 → Q06 → Q07, then Q05/practice. Learns everyday economy, rescue and evidence before descending. May first enter Underfold near promotion.

**Surveyor:** Q05 → Q03 → Q11 → Q04 → Q01 → Q02 plus needed merit. Earns map familiarity, joins approved Q13 with N19, submits environmental observations. The exam tests first aid and judgment, not combat dominance.

**Social party builder:** Q01 → Q09 with peer → Q03 → Q07 → Q06 → Q08, with more work if merit/evidence require. Meets N15/N18, writes fair terms, saves for supplies and can witness an authored N20 clinic-career decision. Not all companions consent to risky jobs.

These are examples, not scripted dependencies. A novice can work alone for most F contracts, while exam companionship is provided. A support aptitude can satisfy route security through tools, distraction and prepared support; final combat implementation must verify solo viability.

## Failure and nonlinearity

A failed first search costs time and modest supplies, not the entire plot. A retreat can lead to Q07 or a reassessed survey. Ambient NPC activity does not consume the Player Contract Pool. Retirement or departure of an optional companion cannot block the exam. Serious misconduct needs clear evidence, review and repair paths.

Do not hide contract expiry behind dialogue time. Tell players when a rest/travel action advances a deadline. Avoid escalating all regional threats merely because they enjoy the tavern.

## What the player should remember

Their first paid report; a cheap blade or focus bought with saved money; a frightened return from uncertainty; a familiar adventurer whose authored life state changed; a familiar clerk's recognition; a promotion earned by judgment. If a playtest remembers only loot rarity and level numbers, the slice has missed its purpose.

## Playtest observations

Record first voluntary job selection, time to first net profit, first upgrade, first party agreement, first honest retreat, noticed NPC change and exam readiness. Ask players to explain rank versus power, their last job's net pay and why a route was unsafe. These are comprehension checks, not promises of broad market demand.
