# Trade, Auction & Market v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Status:** Working cooperative-economy proposal

## Goal

Trading should make other players feel useful, not threatening.

## 1. Three exchange layers

### Common Exchange
Always-available market for:
- materials;
- consumables;
- ordinary equipment;
- crafted goods.

Supports:
- sell listings;
- buy orders;
- quantity;
- search/filter;
- recent price history.

### Specialist Exchange
For:
- rare materials;
- manuals;
- relic components;
- profession tools;
- unusual collectibles.

Can support requested barter categories.

### Curated Auction
For exceptional provenance-rich objects.

Used sparingly so auctions remain special.

## 2. Safe direct trade

Trade window must:

- lock both offers before confirmation;
- visibly reset confirmation after any change;
- show quantity/quality;
- show binding/restriction;
- record transaction;
- support report/dispute evidence.

No last-second swap scams.

## 3. Market fees

Fees are a money sink, but exact values are not locked.

Potential components:
- small listing fee;
- settlement fee;
- premium auction appraisal/registration fee.

Fees must not make low-value beginner trade pointless.

## 4. Price information

Expose:

- recent sale range;
- recent volume;
- current buy orders;
- current sell orders.

Do not expose private player identity by default for common exchange.

## 5. Auction catalogue

Major auctions publish most lots in advance.

Players can:
- watch;
- appraise;
- set budget;
- place proxy bid;
- submit requested barter where allowed.

This removes fixed-hour attendance pressure.

## 6. Anti-monopoly

Progression-essential goods require multiple acquisition routes.

Monitor:
- market share;
- coordinated price control;
- suspicious wash trading;
- hoarding of bottleneck materials.

System can increase alternative supply through world content/NPC procurement rather than spawning unlimited free goods.

## 7. No forced conflict

Avoid:

- open-world PvP over essential nodes;
- first-hit boss ownership;
- loot stealing;
- player-owned toll gates on Main Story routes.

Prefer:
- contribution credit;
- personal/party loot;
- cooperative gathering where appropriate.

## 8. Regional trade

Regions may specialize:

- Bellcross: metalwork;
- Fenwick: river goods;
- Highmere: herbs/wool;
- Istrane ports: imported/specialty goods;
- Ulreth: cold-region resources.

Trade opportunity comes from freight/time/weather, not PvP robbery.

## 9. Item sinks

Peaceful sinks:

- Guild requisition;
- study/disassembly;
- consumables;
- restoration;
- donation/museum;
- expedition equipment.

Beloved named relics should not randomly disappear.

## 10. Social market roles

Players can build reputation as:

- crafter;
- appraiser;
- restorer;
- merchant;
- supplier;
- collector;
- cartographer.

Market success is optional gameplay, not required for Main Story completion.

## 11. Real-money boundary

No real-money bidding for combat power.
No paid auction priority for essential goods.
No hidden paid odds.

Monetization design is outside this document.

## 12. Next validation

Economy simulation should test:

- new-player affordability;
- market liquidity;
- item oversupply;
- inflation;
- cartel vulnerability;
- casual seller usability;
- fees at low/high price points.
