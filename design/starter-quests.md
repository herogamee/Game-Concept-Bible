# Slice contracts — 18 authored jobs

**Status:** Proposed content budget. Rewards are total contract cash before 10% Guild fee. Party shares follow [economy](../world/economy.md). Rank/confidence may be reassessed from evidence.

The six families remain Hunt, Gather, Escort, Explore, Rescue/Search and Investigation. Multi-family work has one primary evidence category to prevent triple-counting a single job toward promotion.

Larger E objectives have explicit party budgets. Field costs must be visible before the player chooses an optional companion.

## Branch and report standard

Every job has success, justified retreat and failure report paths. Only applicable jobs need negotiation or combat; do not write three fake choices that settle identically. Each has one reusable personal beat, one environmental clue and a report response. A valid report can acknowledge incomplete evidence; positive conduct can coexist with reduced cash.

Client-owned delivery/medical items are not free sale loot. Contracts cannot be settled twice through both conversation and the board. If a client changes a hazard estimate, update the version of the posted job and show why. The player retains the accepted version for disputes.

## Availability and qualification

Initially post Q01, Q03, Q04 and Q05 plus safe Q12; Q02 can also be discovered from a conversation. Q06–Q11 emerge from player reports and authored world/action milestones. Player-facing contract instances are not consumed by ambient NPC adventurers. NPC Narrative Activity can reference separate off-screen work without changing the availability of these core progression contracts.

E contracts Q13–Q18 require E license or a specific permitted F party under [rank rules](../guild/rank-system.md). Q15 being located on floor 1 does not make its full paid survey automatically F; the free marked orientation loop has a narrower responsibility. F emergency supply tasks never become compulsory D combat.

Six varied F contracts plus practice should provide around 30 merit. Example evidence path Q01/Q02/Q03/Q04/Q06/Q07 totals **29**; one additional Q05 brings **33**, so the six-contract minimum is not a guarantee of the separate merit requirement. Alternative Q02/Q03/Q04/Q06/Q07/Q08 totals **30** across four families. Review boards explain both counters.

## Player/NPC boundary

The 18 templates form the **Player Contract Pool** for the slice. Ambient or story adventurers may discuss other assignments, leave town or return from off-screen work, but this presentation does not reserve, settle or delete these player-facing contract instances. Companion NPCs participate only when accompanying a player.

## Content boundaries

18 authored templates, two regional event chains, one exam and one optional rumor/sample chain. No procedural endless faction campaign. First-floor discovery may unlock an archive entry or safer route, not a legendary class, chosen-one memory or final-dungeon revelation.
