# Signature Gear & Equipment Legacy v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**Status:** Long-term ownership proposal

## Player promise

> If you love a piece of equipment, the game should usually give you a meaningful path to keep it relevant.

This does not mean every starter sword becomes mathematically best-in-slot.

It means replacement is not the only form of progression.

## 1. Becoming Signature

A gear item earns Signature eligibility from meaningful history, not repetitive farming.

Candidate evidence:
- time actively equipped;
- varied contracts;
- important expedition;
- repair/restoration;
- commissioned modification;
- compatible technique mastery;
- notable discovery;
- Named Monster survival;
- social/crafting history.

No single counter should be sufficient.

## 2. Personal familiarity

Familiarity belongs to player + item relationship.

Possible benefits:
- slightly cleaner recovery;
- faster stance transition;
- lower handling penalty;
- technique-specific comfort.

It must remain bounded so a new weapon is still usable.

## 3. Physical legacy

Craft history can change the item:

- reforged guard;
- new shaft;
- replacement grip;
- Blue Iron reinforcement;
- Pattern fitting;
- specialist material.

Changes preserve old provenance entries.

## 4. Story legacy

Record notable moments such as:

- "Carried during the Siltwell Survey"
- "Repaired by [crafter]"
- "Used in the first verified Choir Vault expedition"

Avoid automatically recording thousands of trivial kills.

## 5. Naming

A player may propose a name after Signature recognition.

Possible validation:
- length/filter;
- no impersonation/system-reserved names;
- name appears in personal provenance first.

World-wide historical recognition requires exceptional events, not simply pressing Rename.

## 6. Retirement

Retired Signature Gear can be:

- displayed at home;
- placed in Guild exhibit if eligible;
- stored in personal collection;
- passed/traded if system rules allow;
- used for training/ceremony.

Retirement should feel respectful, not deletion.

## 7. Transfer

Working proposal:

When traded:
- physical modifications remain;
- provenance remains;
- previous owners remain in history;
- personal familiarity does not transfer;
- new owner builds a new relationship.

This allows a real secondary market for storied equipment without selling character mastery.

## 8. Relic intersection

A Signature item can later become historically important.

But Signature != Relic.

Relic status depends on world/history/function.
Signature status depends on owner relationship/history.

## 9. Anti-exploit

Do not allow:
- AFK equipped-time farming to create prestige;
- trivial mob kill count to force Signature;
- wash-trading to manufacture provenance;
- paid renaming into historical status;
- destroying gear during enhancement failure.

## 10. Example lifetime

Fine Bellcross Sword:
- purchased at E;
- repaired after rescue at D;
- Blue Iron reinforced at C;
- technique synergy at B;
- Pattern fitting after hidden realm at A;
- historically recognized after S expedition.

At S it may still lose raw-stat comparisons to another specialist weapon.

The value is that its history and handling create a distinct choice.
