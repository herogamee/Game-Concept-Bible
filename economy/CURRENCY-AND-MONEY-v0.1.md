# Currency & Money v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Status:** Working system proposal — not final balance

## Goal

Money must matter from Rank F through post-S without turning into giant meaningless numbers.

## 1. Ordinary currency

Keep the established readable coin system:

- 100 Copper = 1 Silver
- 100 Silver = 1 Gold

Copper handles daily life.
Silver handles professional adventurer life.
Gold handles expensive property, major equipment, high-end services and institutional transactions.

Do not add Platinum merely because veteran balances become large. Fix sources/sinks first.

## 2. Money sources

Currency may enter through:

- Guild contracts;
- NPC procurement;
- regional public works;
- selling ordinary goods to NPC demand;
- treasure liquidation;
- limited event rewards.

Player-to-player sales do not create currency.

## 3. Money sinks

Money leaves through:

- market settlement fees;
- auction fees;
- Guild escrow;
- appraisal;
- repair/restoration;
- travel/freight;
- lodging;
- storage expansion;
- housing;
- crafting-station services;
- archive copies;
- expedition preparation;
- cosmetic services.

Every sink must provide understandable value.

## 4. Functional magical resource

Future working class: **Pattern Shard** — placeholder name.

It is NOT ordinary money.

Possible uses:

- Pattern Magic devices;
- relic restoration;
- advanced crafting;
- sealed-node activation;
- expedition wards.

It may be traded, creating a meaningful sell/use/keep decision.

Do not copy cultivation stone names or tier structures.

## 5. Non-money progression

Never convert these directly into tradeable cash:

- Guild Rank;
- faction trust;
- discovery credit;
- technique mastery;
- story access;
- reputation.

This limits pay-to-win and market capture.

## 6. Banking

Banks may provide:

- coin storage;
- account ledger;
- Guild escrow;
- auction settlement;
- optional letters of credit for very large institutional payments later.

Ordinary players should not need finance knowledge to buy food or gear.

## 7. Inflation dashboard

Server telemetry should track:

- coin created/day;
- coin destroyed/day;
- created/destroyed ratio;
- median active-player wealth;
- Rank-F purchasing power;
- commodity basket index;
- market velocity;
- concentration of wealth;
- top-item price movement.

Do not balance only from the richest 1%.

## 8. Anti-inflation rule

If coin creation persistently exceeds destruction:

1. identify the source;
2. adjust systemic sinks or source rewards carefully;
3. protect new-player purchasing power;
4. avoid emergency blanket taxes.

## 9. No mandatory daily economy

No daily-login interest.
No offline wealth decay.
No essential fixed-hour market event.

## 10. Open balance questions

Final numbers still require simulation for:

- contract income;
- repair cost;
- travel cost;
- listing fee;
- auction fee;
- housing upkeep;
- NPC price floors/ceilings.
