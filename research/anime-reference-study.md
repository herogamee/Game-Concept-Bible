# Reference study — transferable motives, original expression

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Scope is a premise/reference analysis, not a claim to have reread every volume or watched every adaptation. v0.1 §22 supplied the study themes; this pass checks public publisher/official premises where accessible and connects them to original design experiments.

“Potential appeal” below is **our interpretation**, not a measured fan consensus. Do not treat it as a fact about every fan. Titles are references only; no names, scenes, characters, logos, costumes, dialogue, maps or proprietary power-system implementation are assets for our game. Solo Leveling is a Korean-origin reference in the user's anime-viewing list, not a Japanese-origin property.

## Study matrix

| Reference | Potential appeal / design interpretation | Original application to test | Boundary against imitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goblin Slayer | Preparation gives apparently small threats consequence | Route warnings, safe retreat, truthful field reports | No protagonist analogue, copied monster scenes, armor silhouette or signature tactics |
| DanMachi | A dungeon gives an inhabited city economic meaning | Ternhaven's repair, lodging, supplies and gate work | No copied city layout, deity household structure or proprietary status system |
| KonoSuba | Social downtime and imperfect teammates can make a Guild memorable | Peer disagreements, cheap meals, comic administrative mishaps | No counterpart party, copied jokes or humiliating archetype replacements |
| Grimgar | Weakness, wages and mutual dependence give early victories weight | Starter budget, loan kit, shared terms and first aid | No copied memory-loss premise, cast or traumatic sequence |
| Frieren | History and personal time make places and qualifications meaningful | Archivist records, veteran adaptations and practical evidence-based assessment | No copied exam stages, long-lived lead, spell names or demon logic |
| Solo Leveling | Readable ranks make disparity and future possibility immediately legible | Professional license shows trust separately from capability | No copied hunter association, private leveling interface, shadow powers or overpowered early acceleration |
| Sword Art Online / Aincrad | Numbered depths imply mystery, milestones and anticipation | Distinct drainage/root/weir floors; preserve ten-strata foundation | No trapped-game premise, copied floor layouts, boss designs or distinctive interface |
| Mushoku Tensei | Travel feels consequential when societies have practical differences | Trade goods, regional charters, costly routes and forwarding letters | No copied reincarnation story, characters, languages or continents |
| Re:Zero | Relationships and decisions can retain emotional consequences | Injuries, disputed reports, changed greetings and recovery paths | No return-by-death mechanism or duplicated dramatic scenes |
| That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime | Negotiated faction interests can make nonhuman societies understandable | Reedkin water rights and asymmetric alliances | No settlement-ruler player role, copied species hierarchy or power acquisition |
| Overlord | Different scales of authority create perspective and political pressure | Branch master, merchants and crown exert limited, conflicting powers | No dominant ruler premise, counterpart cast, copied hierarchy or skill nomenclature |
| Shangri-La Frontier | Discovery feels rewarding when clues and conditions are coherent | Rumor → environmental clue → verified archive entry | No copied unique scenarios, avatars or exploit mechanics; no impossible hidden trigger without clues |
| Dungeon Meshi | Resources and food link creatures to a functioning habitat | Gels consume runoff; insects follow roots; supply weights matter | No copied recipes, creature anatomy or party journey; full monster cooking is outside slice |
| Made in Abyss | Descent has emotional force when logistics and return matter | Return markers, caches, light and temporary shelters | No copied curse, relic taxonomy, visual motifs or child-explorer premise |
| A Record of a Mortal's Journey to Immortality | An ordinary starting position can make careful long progression satisfying | Work, training, savings and credible qualification | No copied cultivation realms, sect names, artifacts or protagonist advantage |
| Battle Through the Heavens | Milestones, scarce preparation and craft practice can reward persistence | Calibrated reagents and bounded advanced-training hooks | No copied alchemy ranks, special flames, techniques or cultivation ladder |
| Soul Land | Team identity and complementary strengths can create attachment | Practical roles, fair agreement, changing party relationships | No copied spirit/ring system, academy, tournament or character powers |
| Renegade Immortal | Long growth can alter judgment and attachment, not just damage | A novice's priorities can evolve into mentorship, specialization or retirement | No copied immortality realms, revenge sequence, artifacts or metaphysics |

The proposed Guild, names, professions and ecological combination are authored for this repository. Broad motifs do not by themselves establish originality clearance; final visual and commercial naming review must examine concrete assets and branding when they exist.

## Community observations and their limits

[J-Novel Club recommendation discussion](https://forums.j-novel.club/topic/7004/asking-for-recommendations/7?lang=en-US) includes a reader who enjoyed Grimgar's beginning but found later material too dreary/strange. [Donghua discussion comparing Soul Land and Battle Through the Heavens](https://www.reddit.com/r/Donghua/comments/1er2nbk) contains disagreement about progression and character development. These are individual reactions, not rankings, national preferences or proof of adaptation quality.

They suggest a **design question**: how can progression retain understandable goals and changing relationships instead of adding only new power tiers? Our answer is a hypothesis—competence, civic recognition and evolving peers—tested in a bounded F/E slice. We do not import cultivation as the professional Guild ladder.

## Translation from references to this IP

Reference motif → intended player feeling → original world cause → bounded mechanic → playtest question. Example: vulnerability → value preparation → harvest road with changing drainage → limited supplies and justified retreat → “did you understand the risk and choose differently?” This chain prevents a collage of copied content disguised by renamed nouns.

If the prototype is fun, deepen the most relevant references through a documented reading/viewing session and broader fan interviews. Do not hold the original draft hostage to consuming every episode, and do not call that future work completed now.
