# Alchemy, Apothecary & Expedition Medicine v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Status:** Working profession proposal

## Fantasy

Alchemy is preparation and material knowledge, not an endless stack of mandatory combat buffs.

## 1. Product families

- stabilization;
- antidotes;
- recovery support;
- environmental resistance;
- preservation;
- diagnostic reagent;
- Pattern-interference preparation;
- specialist solvent/adhesive.

## 2. Ingredient model

Ingredients have properties such as:
- active family;
- potency;
- stability;
- toxicity;
- freshness;
- solvent compatibility.

Recipes can allow substitutions.

A skilled apothecary understands why a substitute works.

## 3. Preparation choices

Potential process:
- identify;
- clean;
- dry/crush;
- extract;
- combine;
- stabilize;
- package.

Routine known formulas become quick craft.

Experimental formulas retain deeper interaction.

## 4. Medicine rules

- no instant full-heal reset;
- no mandatory pre-fight potion stack;
- no addictive daily consumption loop;
- powerful preparations have clear purpose and limits;
- dangerous side effects are communicated.

## 5. Existing products integration

Current catalogue examples:
- Field Stabilizer;
- Reed Antivenom;
- Deepbreath Tonic;
- Coldmarch Draught;
- Clear-Eye Wash;
- Pattern-Calm Salts;
- River Purification Kit;
- Preservation Wax.

## 6. Discovery

New formulas may come from:
- field research;
- healer/apothecary mentors;
- regional knowledge;
- monster ecology;
- old manuals;
- hidden realms.

## 7. Quality

Quality affects:
- stability;
- shelf life;
- consistency;
- dosage efficiency.

Avoid simply doubling effect power.

## 8. Social value

Apothecaries can:
- fill expedition orders;
- support clinics;
- respond to regional outbreaks/toxins;
- identify unknown samples.

This creates noncombat prestige.
