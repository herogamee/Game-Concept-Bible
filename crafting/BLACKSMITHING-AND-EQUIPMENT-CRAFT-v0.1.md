# Blacksmithing & Equipment Craft v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Status:** Working profession proposal

## Fantasy

The blacksmith is not a vending machine for level-scaled swords.

They understand metal, heat, geometry, repair and the needs of a specific owner.

## 1. Work types

- forge new weapon/tool;
- armor component;
- repair;
- regrind/rebalance;
- reinforce;
- replace component;
- reforge;
- specialist alloy;
- restoration support.

## 2. Craft plan

Weapon commission selects:
- family;
- intended role;
- material;
- geometry;
- balance;
- finish;
- optional modification.

Examples:
- spear optimized for brace vs mobile thrust;
- sword biased toward control vs committed cutting;
- hammer optimized for tool/structural use vs combat.

## 3. Material property examples

Bellcross Blue Iron:
- resilient;
- repair-friendly;
- moderately heavy.

Starfall Nickel:
- specialist alloy ingredient;
- difficult processing;
- research value.

Old-World Ceramic:
- specialist heat/Pattern component;
- not a normal blade material.

## 4. Reforging

Reforging changes an existing item while preserving provenance.

Possible:
- geometry;
- guard;
- balance;
- reinforcement;
- damaged component.

Reforging is not "reset item to new tier."

## 5. Masterwork

Masterwork requires demonstrated control and appropriate inputs.

A Masterwork is:
- exceptionally executed;
- identifiable by maker;
- valuable;
- still an ordinary crafted object unless history later makes it a Relic.

## 6. Signature Gear support

Blacksmiths are central to long-lived equipment.

A repair or reforge can become a provenance event.

The craft system must preserve:
- original maker;
- later restorer;
- changed components;
- major expedition history.

## 7. Scrap loop

Damaged ordinary equipment can be:
- repaired;
- sold used;
- disassembled;
- requisitioned;
- recycled into partial material.

No 100% material recovery.

## 8. Anti-junk progression

Repeated low-difficulty crafts give sharply diminishing mastery value.

Better progression:
- new geometry;
- commissions;
- unusual repair;
- new material;
- mentor challenge;
- Guild quality requirement.
