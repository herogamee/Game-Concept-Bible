# Cooking, Food & Expedition Provisioning v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Status:** Working profession proposal

## Goal

Food should make the world feel lived-in and support preparation without becoming a compulsory buff spreadsheet.

## 1. Food roles

- daily meal;
- travel ration;
- recovery meal;
- climate-specific food;
- festival/social food;
- specialist expedition provision.

## 2. Everyday nutrition

Ordinary meals satisfy normal travel/rest needs.

Players should not need to maintain five simultaneous food buffs.

## 3. Prepared meal benefit

A good meal can provide bounded contextual benefits such as:
- improved recovery after rest;
- cold/heat comfort;
- longer ration efficiency;
- morale/social interaction;
- minor expedition readiness.

## 4. Ingredients

Ingredients come from:
- farming/NPC supply;
- fishing;
- foraging;
- hunting/monster ecology where appropriate;
- regional trade.

Avoid making every monster edible.

## 5. Recipes as culture

Recipes record:
- region;
- family/inn tradition;
- festival;
- expedition history.

Learning a dish can itself be travel content.

## 6. Quality

Cooking quality affects:
- taste;
- preservation;
- serving efficiency;
- social/commission value.

Do not make Masterwork soup a mandatory raid DPS buff.

## 7. Group cooking

Camps and parties can share meals.

Benefits should encourage cooperation without punishing solo players.

## 8. Commerce

Cooks can supply:
- inns;
- caravans;
- Guild expeditions;
- festivals;
- players.

Perishability can exist selectively, but do not create inventory anxiety for all food.

## 9. Collection

Recipe book can become a major casual collection:
- regional dishes;
- rare fish dishes;
- historical recipes;
- festival dishes.
