# Willowbrook art direction and generation prompts

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, not the CLI/API fallback. All four source sheets are preserved under `assets/painted/`. The tool returned 1254×1254 RGBA sheets; extraction scripts use actual image dimensions. The requested dimensions below describe the prompt, not a guarantee about tool output.

Palette: cream plaster, walnut timber, orange/teal roofs, sage foliage, teal capes, warm gold highlights. Orthographic 3/4 fantasy village with original characters and recognizable material textures. Individual runtime sprites use small pixel dimensions and nearest-neighbor display. Artwork is illustrated/painterly rather than strict limited-palette pixel art.

## Environment prompt

Production game asset, stylized-concept. Create an ORIGINAL highly polished hand-painted pixel fantasy village prop ATLAS, transparent RGBA background, exactly 1024x1024 pixels, 4 columns by 4 rows of invisible 256x256 cells. One isolated prop centered in EACH cell with 16px transparent padding, no overlapping cells, no text, no labels, no grid lines. Orthographic top-down 3/4 view consistent with old charming 2D fantasy MMORPGs, original IP. Crisp deliberate pixel clusters at 2px resolution, detailed shaded edges, soft warm sunlight upper left, sage foliage, aged cream plaster, walnut timber, terracotta tiles, blue-green water highlights, subtle contact shadow. Row1 cells: elaborate cottage with orange tiled roof chimney flower boxes door; large rounded oak tree with organic layered leaf clusters trunk roots; mossy faceted gray boulder; lush rounded flowering bush. Row2: tiny wildflower cluster; wooden picket fence section straight horizontal; wooden directional sign on pole; circular stone village well with pitched tiled roof. Row3: wrought iron glowing lantern on wooden pole; second distinct cottage with teal roof and timber walls; small market stall red canvas awning baskets; decorative flower planter stone border. Row4: clump of tall grass; wooden barrel and burlap sack; tiny wooden bridge horizontal; rune stone. Readable detailed silhouettes, not simplistic rectangle boxes. All objects face southeast with vertical walls but tops visible. No characters, no scenery background. Assets must be directly usable individually in a playable game.

## Adventurer prompt

Use case stylized-concept, PRODUCTION ORIGINAL GAME SPRITE SHEET. Exactly square 1024x1024 RGBA transparent. An 8 columns × 8 rows evenly spaced invisible grid of 128x128 cells, one SAME small chibi fantasy swordsman in each cell. Consistent character identity: tousled chestnut hair, cream shirt, teal-blue short cape/scarf, brown leather vest and belt pouch, dark boots, small silver sword. Charming sophisticated 2D fantasy RPG pixel art, 3/4 view, deliberate crisp pixel clusters with shaded hair and richly detailed clothing, warm highlights upper left; no logos, no text, no grid, no background. Each character about 72px wide × 90px tall, within cell with generous transparent margin, feet always on same baseline 108px from cell top, center 64px from cell left, never cross cells. Rows 1-4 = eight sequential WALK frames (alternating steps in a fluid full cycle), row1 facing down/front, row2 facing left, row3 facing right, row4 facing up/back. Rows 5-8 = eight sequential SWORD ATTACK frames, row5 facing down/front, row6 left, row7 right, row8 up/back; sequence ready, windup, windup, slash, slash, followthrough, recover, ready. Sword follows facing direction with readable action, clothing sways, stable camera and scale. First walk frame can serve as idle. Absolutely all 64 characters must be complete, same proportions and the same uniform and hair. Not a concept illustration: directly sliceable animation asset, no environment. Original character unrelated to any existing game's characters.

Each cell contains a complete isolated chubby pear-shaped lime-green forest slime with tiny dark oval eyes, cute small mouth, translucent gel lit upper left, small leafy sprout on head, delicate dappled gold highlights, pixel-art hand-painted look consistent with detailed fantasy village and teal-caped adventurer. Consistent identity scale center in every frame, feet baseline cell y200, body about160px wide and120px tall. Row1 4 idle animation frames gentle breathing/squash; row2 4 movement frames bounce squash stretch; row3 4 hit/recoil frames; row4 4 death sequence frames dissolving gel and gold sparkles. Always within the same cell, generous transparent margins. View orthographic top-down 3/4. Detailed curved silhouettes and rich shaded pixel clusters, not a flat geometric blob. Original IP, no copied commercial designs. This must be an actual game-ready animated sheet with clearly separated cells.

## NPC prompt

Original fantasy RPG production NPC sprite sheet. Transparent RGBA, square 1024x1024, STRICT invisible 3 columns × 3 rows, nine isolated complete full-body sprites, no text, labels, grid or background. Match polished hand-painted pixel fantasy MMORPG art: highly detailed chibi anatomy, warm light upper left, colored outline, soft shadows, expressive faces, no hard rectangle body parts. All characters facing down/front in top-down three-quarter camera, feet at same baseline inside cell, full character centered, no overlap, each approximately 180px tall with at least60px padding. Column1 SAME elderly village elder, white eyebrows beard, sage/olive long coat, cream scarf, wooden walking staff; three subtly different idle breathing poses top to bottom. Column2 SAME cheerful merchant, round russet hat, cream blouse, red-brown vest and apron, leather belt purse; three breathing idle poses top to bottom. Column3 SAME veteran female adventurer, auburn braid, deep green cloak, leather tunic, small sword strapped to back; three breathing idle poses top to bottom. Rich fabrics and material textures, rounded silhouettes, distinct identities, grounded feet. Original unrelated to any commercial game's characters. Sprite sheet game asset, not a scene or illustration.

## Runtime and follow-up

- Source sheets are not fetched by the game and are excluded from dist.
- Environment sprite dimensions preserve the previous collider geometry; new market/barrels/rune also have collision.
- Hero is packed as 64×64 frames, slime as 48×48 frames and NPCs as 64×64 frames. Image decoding finishes before the game loop starts.
- Maps still use the same coordinates and save key. The camera now presents an 800×450 logical viewport within the 1280×720 canvas and leaves more room above the character.
- The boar retains the earlier authored sprite in this pass. Terrain remains deterministic code art; the focus was one polished village, adventurer and slime set.
- This is an integrated art-quality pass, not a claim of finished commercial-game art. Further manual cleanup can improve frame-to-frame silhouette and palette consistency.
