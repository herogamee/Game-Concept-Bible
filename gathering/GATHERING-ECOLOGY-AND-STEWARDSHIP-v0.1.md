# Gathering, Ecology & Resource Stewardship v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Status:** Working gathering proposal

## Thesis

> A skilled gatherer knows where, when and how to harvest — not merely which pickaxe tier can hit the node.

## 1. Paths

### Mining
Ore, stone, crystals, old deposits.

### Herbalism
Medicinal plants, fibers, resins.

### Forestry
Wood, bark, sap, specialist growth.

### Fishing
Food, specimens, rare ecological discoveries.

### Foraging
Food, fungi, seeds, minor materials.

### Salvage
Ruins, damaged equipment, old infrastructure — with ownership/legal rules.

### Monster-material fieldwork
Shed, remains, samples, ethical/nonlethal harvest where possible.

## 2. Discovery

Resource sites can be found by:
- terrain;
- plant signs;
- geology;
- animal behavior;
- weather;
- local knowledge;
- maps;
- profession techniques.

## 3. Harvest interaction

The player chooses:
- what to take;
- how much;
- extraction method;
- whether to preserve specimen quality.

Skilled work can trade quantity for quality.

## 4. Regeneration

Common resources replenish predictably.

Rare resources may depend on:
- season;
- ecology;
- weather;
- world event;
- hidden realm.

No essential progression resource should depend on a single globally exclusive spawn.

## 5. Ecology

Harvest pressure can change local authored/systemic states, but effects are bounded and recoverable.

Examples:
- herb shortage creates Guild conservation contract;
- overharvest lowers quality temporarily;
- responsible gathering earns specialist reputation.

## 6. Gatherer knowledge journal

Records:
- location family;
- conditions;
- uses;
- quality signs;
- safe extraction;
- discovered substitutes.

Knowledge is progression.

## 7. Collectable specimens

Some finds are better donated/studied than processed:
- unusual ore sample;
- rare plant;
- fish specimen;
- ancient manufactured material.

Rewards:
- research;
- reputation;
- archive entry;
- cosmetic/display;
- recipe clue.

## 8. Anti-bot / anti-monopoly direction

Do not solve bots by making normal players miserable.

Use server-side behavioral detection and economy telemetry.

Design-side protections:
- distributed sources;
- substitutions;
- personal/party expedition resources where needed;
- bounded node competition;
- no first-click ownership of critical materials.

## 9. Casual sessions

A 20-minute player can:
- fish;
- gather herbs;
- survey a deposit;
- fill a small commission;
- update journal.

Gathering must be valid primary play.
