# Magic foundation — bounded pattern working

**Status:** Proposed cosmology and limits. Combat model and final spell lists remain open.

## Source, learning and expenditure

Ambient **drift** is a working term for low-density magical energy shaped by terrain and buried structures. A trained pattern directs existing energy into a bounded effect. Living casters supply attention and bodily effort; a focus stores calibration, not unlimited fuel. Materials stabilize effects and wear out.

Learning requires instruction, practice and a demonstrated safe discharge. Books explain patterns but do not grant mastery by opening a menu. A farm laborer can learn a minor drying charm; a full mage learns diagnosis, counter-patterns and environmental assessment. Talent helps practice but does not establish a chosen caste able to do everything.

Proposed casting checks: valid learned pattern, focus condition, fatigue budget, required reagent, reachable target, local stability. Failure can cancel a spell, crack a focus or create a visible minor hazard. The first slice uses predictable failures with advance UI signals; it does not randomly maim new casters.

## Non-negotiable limits within this proposal

- Magic redirects or accelerates processes; it cannot create unlimited grain, ore, currency or fresh water from nothing.
- Range, area and duration raise effort and material cost. A bucket-scale drying charm is not flood control.
- Healing requires tissue, nutrition and time. It can stabilize bleeding and support recovery; it does not reset all consequences.
- Reliable long-distance teleportation, resurrection and instantaneous global communication are absent from the slice and unapproved for the final game.
- Knowable interference matters: mineral seams, damaged old patterns and weather can reduce reliability. Show signs and offer diagnosis rather than arbitrary spell denial.
- Training and reagents are scarce enough to preserve physical crafts, couriers and escorts.

## Practice families

| Practice | Useful low-rank application | Limit / cost | World occupation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heat exchange | Dry a bandage, light prepared tinder | Must transfer heat; overuse chills caster or cracks vessel | Kitchens, kilns, travel preparation |
| Impulse | Brief shield brace or move a small obstruction | Momentum/reaction load; cannot lift bridges cheaply | Porters and emergency crews |
| Sensing | Detect airflow disturbance, read a marked trail | Short radius, noisy readings; cannot reveal every secret | Surveyors and scouts |
| Binding | Stabilize a rope joint, tune a prepared focus | Finite duration and inspectable anchor | Enchanters, builders |
| Vital care | Stop bleeding after first aid, reduce infection risk | Needs clean supplies; fatigue and food; no universal cure | Clinics and support practitioners |

Offensive magic may adapt these practices later; do not lock damage formulas or combat timing in v0.2. Fighters, rangers and scouts retain tool-based equivalents for core expedition tasks.

## Healing and failure

Every starting aptitude receives ordinary first-aid access. A support mage saves supplies or recovery time within a cap; they do not replace all lodging and medicine. Prototype temporary injury lasts one rest tick by default, at most two for the specified failure chain. Clinic stabilization exists if an adventurer lacks money; it may require a safe follow-up task, not compounding debt.

Player death and permanent NPC death remain open from v0.1. Slice defeat returns the party through an authored rescue outcome with temporary loss and injury. No secret permadeath flag, resurrection fee or irreversible body consequence is introduced.

## Enchanting, items and trade

Enchanting embeds a narrow pattern into a durable anchor with limited charge/condition. An apprentice's lantern resists brief dampness; it is not an everlasting portable sun. Enchanted equipment lists charges, repair requirements and permitted services. Repair needs craft skill and calibration, keeping smiths economically important.

Consumable potion: manufactured reagent plus stabilizer; finite bottle stock; expiry can remain narrative in the slice rather than a second inventory timer. Magic items should provide a distinct practical benefit before a rarity color. No beginner gear invalidates navigation, inventory weight or preparation.

## Forbidden practices and law

Working legal bans cover coercive alteration of memory, unwilling bodily binding, deliberate contamination of civic water and unlicensed manipulation of old pressure works near settlements. These are action-based restrictions, not a blanket ban on a race or element. Local jurisdictions disagree on research exceptions.

Guild confiscation needs evidence and a review record. A discovered dangerous formula can be sealed and reported, researched later under supervision, or illicitly sold. This is future narrative scope; do not include a fully playable coercion/forbidden spell tree in the first slice.

## Prototype budget and questions

Implement three utility patterns and one support pattern at most; use shared condition/reagent interfaces. Every vital quest has a nonmagical route. Open questions: drift's origin, divine intervention, advanced schools, ultimate resurrection policy and final caster resource representation. Tune fatigue and charge counts after combat selection, not through a 200-spell document now.
