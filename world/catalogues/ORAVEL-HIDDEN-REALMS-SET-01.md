# Oravel Hidden Realms Catalogue — Set 01

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Count:** 10
**Status:** Authored exploration proposal

## HR-01 — The Grey Orchard
**Access:** E  
**Region:** Reedmark edge  
**Discovery:** Grey Orchard Seed + old farming records + unusual soil sample.  
**Challenge:** ecology, restoration, no required boss.  
**Reward:** rare plants, cultivation/cooking knowledge, clue to pre-catastrophe agriculture.  
**Return value:** seasonal growth cycles.

## HR-02 — Siltwell's Third Breath
**Access:** E/D  
**Region:** behind known Siltwell chambers  
**Discovery:** airflow discrepancy + survey measurements.  
**Challenge:** cave navigation and pressure changes.  
**Reward:** Ashglass deposits and old maintenance notes.  
**Connection:** proves Siltwell once linked to infrastructure beyond the visible cave.

## HR-03 — The Drowned Reading Room
**Access:** D  
**Region:** Fenwick river system  
**Discovery:** ferryman stories + low-water event + authenticated ledger.

## Multiplayer fairness

Server-first discovery grants history/title prestige.
Once discovered, meaningful narrative access becomes reproducible for other players through personal/party expedition states.
No guild can permanently own these realms.
