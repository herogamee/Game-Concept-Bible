# Hidden Realms & Secret Areas v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Status:** Working exploration proposal

## Thesis

A hidden realm is not a random bonus portal. It is a discoverable adventure with a reason to exist.

## 1. Original realm families

- Sealed Node
- Lost Workshop
- Legacy Ground
- Environmental Pocket
- Spatial Anomaly
- Lost Settlement
- Forgotten Archive

## 2. Discovery chain

> rumor -> evidence -> research -> preparation -> access condition -> expedition -> discovery -> consequence

Multiple clues should usually exist.

## 3. Access ingredients

Possible requirements:

- map fragment;
- language;
- weather/tide;
- Pattern condition;
- relic/key;
- profession knowledge;
- faction permission;
- puzzle;
- route discovery;
- previous quest outcome.

Avoid pure 0.01% entrances.

## 4. Shared-world fairness

First discovery may award:

- title;
- journal credit;
- server history;
- cosmetic memorial.

It must NOT permanently lock all other players out.

Use:
- personal/party story instances;
- reopening cycles;
- equivalent access routes;
- replenishing resource layers.

## 5. Realm reward mix

A realm may reward:

- knowledge;
- technique;
- artifact;
- rare material;
- crafting station;
- NPC relationship;
- map;
- lore;
- collection;
- shortcut.

Not every realm needs a boss.

## 6. Difficulty

Challenge can come from:

- navigation;
- survival;
- puzzle;
- ecology;
- limited supplies;
- Pattern instability;
- social choice;
- combat.

## 7. Return value

Some realms are one-time narrative spaces.
Others can support repeat visits through:

- changing ecology;
- resource cycle;
- deeper sealed sections;
- research;
- crafting;
- seasonal condition.

## 8. Underfold integration

Hidden realms can reveal that the Underfold is part of a larger network without making every secret place an Underfold room.

## 9. Casual access

Important discoveries should include short-form possibilities.

Not every secret requires a four-hour raid group.

## 10. No ownership monopoly

Guilds/players cannot permanently claim progression-essential secret realms as private territory.
