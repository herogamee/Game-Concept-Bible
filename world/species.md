# Peoples / species proposal

**Status:** Open decision. Three candidates, not a mandatory creation-menu roster.

| People | World role and political pressure | Gameplay expression | Constraints |
|---|---|---|---|
| Humans | Numerous valley and upland cultures; varied crafts, farming and institutions | Familiar early embodiment; backgrounds teach local practices | No universal adaptability bonus that makes all other choices inferior |
| Reedkin | Amphibious waterside communities, ferry crews and wetland surveyors; disputes over drainage and settlement rights | Better water reading and short submerged traversal; special route conversations | Dry conditions require care; cannot bypass every river hazard or live without supplies |
| Cairnfolk | Dense-boned upland communities, masonry, surveying and cold-season transport; contested mine leases | Stable load handling and reading structure wear | Climbing speed and fatigue tradeoffs; not an automatic combat tank or compulsory smith |

These peoples are proposed physiological and social groupings; each contains multiple cultures, professions and viewpoints. Avoid copying an existing work's appearance, naming, lifespan, racial stat tree or exclusive power system. Concept art must explore original silhouettes before implementation.

## Why only three?

Each contributes something the starter region needs: food and institutions, waterways, or built infrastructure. Additional species must change relationships or play enough to justify animation, equipment, dialogue and accessibility work. A list of ten cosmetically different peoples would dilute production resources and world clarity.

No innate species morality. Hostile dungeon groups are particular communities or creatures with ecological needs; they are not substitutes for an evil playable ethnicity. The ecology may still contain gels, cave wolves and goblin/kobold/orc categories already outlined in v0.1; whether those categories are sapient peoples and how they are represented remain open. Do not quietly replace that foundation taxonomy.

## Creation and slice implication

Working slice default: human player plus human-rendered persistent adventurers; Reedkin representative via portrait/dialogue. This contains equipment and rig cost while preserving an open final roster. The player chooses a background such as farm laborer, porter or apprentice, each with one practical familiarity and a social connection rather than a heroic origin.

If species become playable, economic or traversal advantages need compensating routes for every other species. Basic navigation and first-aid exam tasks accept tools and learned methods. Never require a paid race-specific item to pass a universal Guild license exam.

## Approval questions for later

Playable versus NPC-only status; physiology and longevity; cosmetic variation; hybrid families; equipment compatibility; traversal fairness; writing and localization terminology. Approval of names does not automatically approve numerical racial bonuses.
