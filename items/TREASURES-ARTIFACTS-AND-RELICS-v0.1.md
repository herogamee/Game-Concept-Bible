# Treasures, Artifacts & Relics v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Status:** Working item-system proposal

## Thesis

> A treasure should create a story, decision or new possibility — not merely a larger stat.

## 1. Treasure Desire Model

Evaluate important treasure across:

- Mystery
- Utility
- Provenance
- Connection
- Choice
- Growth
- Memory

Raw power is intentionally not the only axis.

## 2. Narrative categories

### Curiosity
Unknown significance.

### Specialist Treasure
High value to a profession/build.

### Named Relic
Documented history and identity.

### Legacy Relic
Connected to a person, school, expedition or lost institution.

### World Relic
Connected to major ancient/world systems.

These are not automatic DPS tiers.

## 3. Equipment quality is separate

Normal weapons/armor may use workmanship bands such as:

- Worn
- Serviceable
- Fine
- Masterwork

Final naming/balance remains open.

A Masterwork ordinary sword can be better in combat than a fragile historical relic whose value is archaeological.

## 4. Provenance record

Important objects can store:

- discovered name;
- true name;
- maker/culture;
- era;
- previous owner;
- discovery place;
- finder/party;
- appraisal history;
- restoration history;
- known functions;
- unknown functions;
- linked clue/realm;
- crafted/restored by.

## 5. Identification layers

Properties may be discovered through:

- inspection;
- appraiser;
- profession knowledge;
- Guild archive;
- Pattern analysis;
- field use;
- linked site;
- translation.

Unknown must be discoverable, not arbitrary.

## 6. Relic growth

A beloved relic can improve through:

- restoration;
- missing component;
- translation;
- attunement;
- compatible technique;
- return to origin;
- related fragment.

Avoid endless replacement treadmill.

## 7. Trade classes

Default:
- ordinary gear/materials: freely tradeable;
- most curiosities/relics: tradeable;
- story keys: not sellable when doing so could block progression;
- custody artifacts: special rules explained by story.

Binding must have a clear reason.

## 8. Multiplayer uniqueness

Separate:

- lore uniqueness;
- provenance uniqueness;
- server-first prestige;
- mechanical availability.

One player may own the historically first-discovered original without denying equivalent gameplay to everyone else.

## 9. Original starter relic concepts

### Compass of the Dry River
Detects buried pressure/water flow near old infrastructure.

### Bell of Returning Echoes
Records limited acoustic Pattern traces for investigation.

### Ashglass Needle
Diagnostic instrument for damaged Pattern structures.

### The Unfinished Atlas
Reveals routes after the owner verifies landmarks personally.

### Grey Orchard Seed
Opens a noncombat ecological mystery when brought to the correct environment.

## 10. Collection

Relic journal should preserve acquisition history and allow display in home/Guild museum.

Collection rewards favor:
- knowledge;
- cosmetics;
- display;
- titles;
- story;
rather than mandatory combat power.
