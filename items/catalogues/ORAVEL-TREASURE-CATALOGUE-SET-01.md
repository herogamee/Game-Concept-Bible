## Distribution notes

- F/E treasures teach that utility and history matter.
- D/C treasures connect regional mysteries, Named Monsters and institutions.
- B/A treasures expand toward other landmasses and ancient-network evidence.
- S/post-S treasures ask new questions instead of ending progression.
- No item above is required to be globally unique in mechanical access.
