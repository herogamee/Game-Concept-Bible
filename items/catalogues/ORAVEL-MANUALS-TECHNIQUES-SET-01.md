## Learning rules

- Acquiring a record does not instantly grant mastery.
- Fragments can teach useful partial techniques.
- Common records can be copied.
- Originals retain provenance/collection value.
- Rare does not automatically mean higher damage.
- Techniques can unlock exploration, medicine, craft, investigation or social utility.
