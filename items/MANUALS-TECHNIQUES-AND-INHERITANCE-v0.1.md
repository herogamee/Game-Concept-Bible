# Manuals, Techniques & Inheritance v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Status:** Working progression proposal

## Thesis

Techniques exist in the world. Learning them should itself be an adventure.

## 1. Record types

- Field Notes
- Training Manual
- School Codex
- Fragmented Codex
- Legacy Record
- Pattern Treatise
- Restricted Record

## 2. Learning loop

> acquire -> identify -> understand prerequisite -> study -> practice -> demonstrate -> master -> discover variants

Reading alone does not instantly create mastery.

## 3. Prerequisites

A technique may require:

- weapon familiarity;
- Discipline;
- Pattern knowledge;
- language;
- mentor;
- physical timing;
- another fragment;
- safe training location.

Do not use arbitrary level gates when a world-based prerequisite is better.

## 4. Fragments

A technique may have separately useful components:

- theory;
- stance;
- force transfer;
- execution;
- recovery;
- advanced application.

Incomplete does not mean useless.

## 5. Manuals and copying

Working proposal:

- common manuals: freely copyable;
- specialist manuals: copyable by trained scribes with cost/time;
- Pattern diagrams: copy quality matters;
- historical originals: copyable for knowledge but original retains provenance/collection value;
- custody/restricted records: institutional rules may limit legal copying.

Do not manufacture scarcity by making all knowledge magically soulbound.

## 6. Mentors

Mentors can:

- correct mistakes;
- unlock safe practice;
- teach variants;
- provide context;
- evaluate mastery.

Players should not be forced to wait real-time days for lessons.

## 7. Inheritance

Inheritance can award:

- technique;
- workshop;
- responsibility;
- map;
- artifact custody;
- unfinished mission;
- relationship;
- archive access.

It should create future play, not hand out an ultimate build.

## 8. Dangerous knowledge

Some records can be risky because they are:

- incomplete;
- physically demanding;
- unstable Pattern theory;
- politically restricted;
- based on false assumptions.

Risk must be telegraphed.

## 9. Build diversity

Rare technique ≠ universally stronger.

A rare technique can be:
- narrow;
- difficult;
- utility-focused;
- exploration-focused;
- synergistic with unusual equipment.

## 10. Originality rule

Future Chinese-fantasy research informs the appeal of manuals and inheritance only.

Never copy named arts, cultivation stages, sects or signature training scenes.
