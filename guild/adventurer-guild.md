# Adventurer Compact Guild

**Status:** Proposed institutional detail; preserves v0.1's Guild as a physical professional home.

## Origin and legal reach

After a flood exposed the failures of private rescue arrangements, river towns created a common escrow, map ledger and return-signal compact about 184 years before the campaign. Traveling licenses followed. Today's Guild operates through a cross-border assembly, regional councils and chartered local branches.

Authority is contractual and locally recognized: license verification, contract eligibility, mediation, emergency coordination and professional suspension. Guards and courts retain arrest and criminal jurisdiction. A Guild clerk cannot legally imprison someone solely for leaving a party. Branches can differ in fees or local permits, but may not rewrite the shared F–S ladder without formal charter action.

## Ternhaven Guild Hall

Ground floor circulation: entrance → reception → board → preparation counter → exit toward market/gate. Tavern alcove and party tables flank the board; training yard and infirmary sit behind it. Archive and examination room occupy a quieter upper level. Keep first-time routes short and visible.

| Space | Practical use | Living-world cue |

| Quest board | Ranked posted contracts and updated information | Ambient adventurers discuss work without consuming the player's contract pool |

Rankings publish milestones, verified discoveries and regional commendations; they are not a leaderboard equating rank with DPS. Major new discoveries and prestige during active play are player-centric. No universal live online rankings are required in the offline slice.

## License and contract lifecycle

License contains registry ID, name, rank, branch of issue, permit endorsements, standing, emergency contact and last verification. Public-facing tag exposes rank and ID, not medical records. Credentials transfer between branches after record check; losing a physical tag requires verification/replacement, not losing character growth.

Contract state: Draft → Assessed/Escrowed → Posted → Reserved → Active → Reported → Verified → Settled. Branches can Cancel or Dispute; overdue jobs enter Review before being treated as misconduct. In v0.2.1, player-facing Posted contracts belong to the **Player Contract Pool**. Ambient or story NPC activity is represented separately as **NPC Narrative Activity** and does not remove meaningful player contracts from the board.

Every board entry shows issuer, objective, advertised risk rank, confidence, location, known hazards, party/permit requirements, expected time, gross reward, fee estimate, salvage rights, deadline basis and retreat/report terms. Clients may conceal information; the UI distinguishes their claims from Guild verification. Do not lie invisibly to manufacture surprise.

Accepted player jobs use explicit in-world action ticks; paused time, dialogue and menus do not expire them. The slice board maintains at least one safe paid job and one exploration alternative. Consequential authored jobs may change through player action or explicit world/story milestones, not because autonomous NPC bots consumed the content.

## Rules, rewards and misconduct

Rules: truthful reports; no forged credentials; honor agreed shares; aid civilians when reasonably safe; respect local law and salvage rights; report unanticipated hazards; do not accept work more than one rank above personal license; no compelled service merely because a bell rings.

Rewards separate cash, merit, documented competencies, local standing and individual trust. A rescue with no lucrative salvage can earn strong professional recognition. A powerful combat victory with reckless conduct may earn cash but block promotion pending review.

Punishment ladder: documented warning → restitution for evidenced loss → limited permit restriction → temporary contract suspension → revocation with regional appeal. Criminal acts go to lawful authorities. Penalties require known acts and witnesses; hidden global morality must not punish unwitnessed choices automatically. Reporting a justified retreat is not misconduct.

Accidental failure affects reliability differently from deception. Clients can contest evidence; mediation preserves provisional access to safe work while a beginner disputes a report. No permanent loss of all play routes from a single low-rank misunderstanding.

## Emergencies

Watch: verify warning, add supplies and optional survey work. Alert: close unsafe routes, mobilize rescue-ready parties, post civilian tasks. Crisis: coordinate with guards and regional branches; issue rank-appropriate emergency assignments. Afterward: debrief, compensate and update records.

F emergency roles: supplies, marked-route messages, shelter work, assisted casualty transport. E: safe perimeter checks or supervised rescue approach. Higher ranks handle major threats. Emergencies do **not** bypass the one-rank-above ceiling or draft the player into A-rank combat.

Overdue party flow: grace/check contact → investigate last marker → classify evidence → dispatch suitable party. NPC disappearance can become a search contract. In the slice it resolves to return, temporary injury or retirement; permanent death remains unapproved.

## Politics

Merchant funding may pressure assessors to downgrade road hazards. Nobles may demand salvage secrecy. The Collegium may withhold diagnostics. The branch master must choose priorities with incomplete supplies. Present one concrete disputed contract in the slice; leave national assembly politics for later.

The player can testify, expose falsified reports or earn trust. They do not allocate all branch staffing, set its budget or become Guild Master as the core progression reward.
