# Promotion rules

**Status:** F → E prototype specification; higher-rank exams are outlines only.

## Proposed F → E eligibility

- Six verified meaningful contracts across at least three of v0.1's six quest families.
- At least one verified field report and one documented act of responsible judgment.
- 30 merit, a prototype target independent of XP; ordinary meaningful jobs give 4–6.
- Basic navigation, first aid and threat-recognition training completed or demonstrated.
- No unresolved serious misconduct; accidental failure alone is not a permanent block.

Use distinct objectives, not six repetitions of the same depot task. Recovery work gives money but no new qualification credit. NPC-completed jobs must not make the player unable to assemble enough eligible evidence: the board supplies authored alternatives. Classes or species do not exempt applicants from safety competence.

**Expected pacing:** readiness around hours 7–10 for a varied route; slower play or social exploration can delay it. Time played is not an eligibility condition. Capability growth cannot auto-award E.

## Examination: return with everyone

An independently assigned examiner briefs a supervised field exercise near Aldermead. The test includes a route-choice station, a misidentified hazard, a wounded volunteer and a deliberately tempting optional salvage detour. Use trained volunteers and safe props where possible, not a disposable NPC forced into real injury.

| Station | Evidence of competence | Passing alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation | Water, light when needed, aid supplies and return plan | Borrow basic examination supplies; no expensive gear gate |
| Navigation | Identify two route markers and a fallback shelter | Map/sketch, landmarks or suitable scouting tool |
| Threat judgment | Recognize mismatch in report and avoid reckless engagement | Inspect, report, ask assistance or withdraw from hazard |
| First aid | Stabilize volunteer with correct sequence | Bandage and clean supplies; bounded healing support also acceptable |
| Teamwork | Agree roles, transport casualty and return within safe route | Solo applicant works with examiner-provided companion |
| Debrief | Distinguish observation from inference; disclose abandoned salvage | Honest uncertainty acceptable; fabricated kill claim fails |

Prototype scoring: six stations at 0–2; pass at **9/12**, with preparation, threat judgment and first aid each at least 1. Deliberately abandoning the casualty or falsifying evidence requires a retake regardless of points. Retreat from a real unexpected danger pauses/replaces the test; it is not scripted failure.

No exam fee in the slice. The first retake is available after one relevant practice task/rest tick, with clear feedback on the failed station. Supplies can be lent. Repeated attempts vary clue placement and route, not essential safety rules. Record examiner, scores, reasons and evidence in the license ledger.

## Reward and recognition

On passing: E license, local news entry, one personal greeting from reception, new ordinary E contracts and access to approved floor-2/3 work when prepared. No giant XP multiplier, miracle weapon or instant fame. Current party relationships decide who celebrates, competes or feels left behind.

Players who remain F can still earn money, explore safe routes and progress skills. Rank is desirable access and trust, not the sole way to play.

## Later promotions, preserving v0.1

| Transition | Proposed evidence / test | Major failure mode to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| E → D | Multi-day field exercise, fallback supplies, honest operational report | A mere damage check or farming dozens of identical jobs |
| D → C | Party rescue/investigation with conflicting evidence | Mandatory perfect outcome despite unknowable facts |
| C → B | Command several lower-rank teams and account for safety | Turning ordinary gameplay into full Guild administration |
| B → A | Exceptional achievement, endorsements and hard expedition | Buying recommendations or skipping review with combat level |
| A → S | Historic contribution plus independent senior scrutiny | Infinite point grinding or adding SSS to prolong progression |

Exact merit thresholds, exam durations and appeals remain open for later versions. Specialist endorsements supplement rank; they do not create seven duplicate profession ladders.
